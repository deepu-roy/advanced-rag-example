# Bereavement Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to five (5) paid days of bereavement leave following
the death of an immediate family member, defined as a spouse, domestic
partner, child, parent, sibling, or grandparent. Employees are entitled to
two (2) paid days of bereavement leave for the death of a member of their
extended family, including aunts, uncles, cousins, and in-laws.

Bereavement leave does not need to be taken consecutively, but must be used
within 60 days of the date of death. To take bereavement leave, notify your
manager as soon as reasonably possible; no advance notice is required given
the nature of the leave. People Operations may ask for supporting
documentation, such as an obituary or funeral program, but this is only to
process the leave correctly and is never a condition of approval.

Employees who need more time than this policy provides may use accrued
paid time off, or request an unpaid leave extension through People
Operations. Bereavement leave is paid at the employee's normal daily rate
and does not affect PTO accrual.
