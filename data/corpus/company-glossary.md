# Company Glossary of Terms and Acronyms

This glossary defines acronyms and terms used across Northbridge Freight's
internal documentation, for anyone new to the industry or the company.

- OTIF (On-Time-In-Full): a shipment that arrives on schedule and with its
  complete quantity, used as the primary delivery quality metric.
- POD (Proof of Delivery): a signed or photographed confirmation that a
  shipment was delivered.
- ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival): the predicted delivery time for a
  shipment, updated as tracking events come in.
- SLA (Service Level Agreement): a contracted commitment to a customer
  covering delivery performance and support responsiveness.
- KPI (Key Performance Indicator): a measurable value used to track
  performance against a goal, such as on-time-in-full rate.
- LTL / FTL (Less Than Truckload / Full Truckload): two truck shipping
  modes; LTL shares a truck's capacity across multiple shippers' freight,
  FTL dedicates the whole truck to one shipper.
- EDI (Electronic Data Interchange): a standardized format carriers and
  customers use to exchange shipment and invoice data automatically.
- WMS / TMS (Warehouse / Transportation Management System): the internal
  platforms covering warehouse operations and shipment/carrier
  coordination, respectively.
- RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization): an approval number required
  before a shipment can be returned or redirected.

This document is a quick reference only; each acronym's operational
process (how to file something, who approves it) is documented separately
in its own runbook or policy page.
