# Sabbatical Leave Policy

Employees who have completed five (5) or more continuous years of service
are eligible for a paid sabbatical of up to six (6) weeks, taken once every
five years of service thereafter. Sabbatical leave is paid at 100% of base
salary and does not draw down an employee's PTO balance.

Sabbaticals must be requested at least 90 days in advance through People
Operations and are subject to manager approval based on team coverage
during the requested window. Unlike PTO, sabbatical time cannot be split
into non-consecutive periods — it must be taken as a single continuous
block of up to six weeks.

Employees returning from sabbatical are guaranteed reinstatement to their
same role, or a comparable role if their prior position has changed
significantly during their absence. Sabbatical eligibility resets on the
return date, so the next sabbatical becomes available five years after the
employee returns to work, not five years after the leave began.
