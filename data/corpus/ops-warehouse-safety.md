# Warehouse Safety Guidelines

All warehouse staff must wear steel-toed boots, a high-visibility vest, and
safety glasses when on the warehouse floor. Forklift operation requires a
current internal certification, renewed annually, and forklifts must not
exceed 5 mph anywhere in the facility.

Pallets stacked above four levels must be shrink-wrapped and secured with
strapping before being moved. Any spill, regardless of size, must be
reported to a shift lead immediately and cordoned off before cleanup
begins; unattended spills are the single most common cause of warehouse
injury.

Every warehouse conducts a mandatory safety walk-through at the start of
each shift, logged by the shift lead. Near-miss incidents — an event that
could have caused injury but didn't — must be reported through the same
system as actual incidents, since near-miss data is what the safety team
uses to catch problems before someone gets hurt.
