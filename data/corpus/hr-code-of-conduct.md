# Code of Conduct and Vendor Gifts

Northbridge Freight expects every employee to act with honesty and avoid
conflicts of interest when dealing with carriers, vendors, and customers.
This document covers the most common question employees ask: what can I
accept from a vendor?

Employees may accept a gift from a vendor or carrier only if it is worth
less than $50 and is not cash, a cash equivalent, or a gift card. Meals are
treated the same way: a vendor may buy you a single modest meal in the
course of normal business, but a pattern of frequent paid meals from the
same vendor must be reported to your manager.

Gifts worth $50 or more must be politely declined, or if that is not
possible without causing offense (for example, a gift left at a front
desk), must be turned in to People Operations, logged, and either returned
or raffled off internally. Under no circumstances may an employee accept a
gift, discount, or favor in exchange for steering business toward a
particular vendor or carrier.

Any employee who is unsure whether something crosses the line should ask
their manager or People Operations before accepting it, not after. Failure
to disclose a gift that should have been reported is treated as a conduct
violation, separate from the gift itself.
