# VPN Access Policy

All access to internal systems from outside the corporate office network
must go through the company's virtual private network client, Northbridge
Connect, which is built on the WireGuard protocol. Employees and
contractors may not use split-tunnel configurations, personal VPN
services, or direct exposure of internal systems to the public internet as
a substitute.

Full-time employees receive Northbridge Connect access automatically as
part of onboarding. Contractors and external partners must be sponsored by
a Northbridge employee and require additional approval before a VPN
account is provisioned — see the access approval process for the
sponsorship and sign-off steps.

VPN sessions time out after 12 hours of inactivity and require
re-authentication through single sign-on. Employees traveling to a country
on the company's restricted list must notify IT Security before travel, as
VPN access may be temporarily suspended or routed differently for security
reasons while traveling in those regions.
