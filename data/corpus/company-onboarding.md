# New Employee Onboarding Overview

Every new employee's first week follows the same structure regardless of
role: day one covers paperwork, laptop setup, and building access; day two
covers company-wide orientation, including the code of conduct and
benefits enrollment; days three through five are role-specific onboarding
led by the new hire's manager and team.

New employees are assigned an onboarding buddy from their team, distinct
from their manager, whose job is to answer the small day-to-day questions
that don't warrant a meeting. IT provisions a laptop, email, and baseline
system access before day one; role-specific system access (VPN,
production systems, customer data) is requested by the manager during the
first week and follows the standard access approval process.

Benefits enrollment must be completed within 30 days of the start date, or
enrollment is delayed until the next open enrollment period. New employees
are automatically enrolled in the company's default PTO and sick leave
accrual from their first day.
