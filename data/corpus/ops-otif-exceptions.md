# On-Time-In-Full Exception Filing Process

When a shipment fails to arrive on-time-in-full — meaning it either arrives
late, arrives with missing units, or both — the assigned carrier ops
coordinator must file an exception within 24 hours of the delivery window
closing. Filing late does not void the exception, but it does move it out
of the current billing cycle's reconciliation batch and into the next one.

To file an exception, open the shipment record in the transportation
management system and select "File Delivery Exception." Attach the proof
of delivery document (or note its absence), select a root-cause category
—carrier delay, warehouse short-pick, customer refusal, or damage— and
enter a brief description. The system automatically notifies the account
manager for shipments above $10,000 in declared value.

Exceptions are reviewed weekly by the ops leadership team, and any carrier
with an on-time-in-full rate below 92% over a rolling 30-day window is
flagged for a performance review. A carrier that fails two consecutive
performance reviews is placed on probationary status, during which new
shipments are only assigned to them as a last resort.
