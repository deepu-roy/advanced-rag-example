# Jury Duty Leave Policy

Employees summoned for jury duty are entitled to up to ten (10) paid days
of jury duty leave per calendar year. If jury service extends beyond ten
days, additional days are unpaid unless the employee applies accrued PTO
to cover them. Jury duty leave does not affect PTO accrual or any other
benefit.

To take jury duty leave, provide People Operations with a copy of the jury
summons as soon as it is received, and again with proof of service
(typically a certificate provided by the court) after the leave is
complete. Employees are expected to report to work on any day they are
excused from jury service with more than half the working day remaining.

Employees who are unsure whether a related obligation qualifies — for
example, being subpoenaed as a witness rather than summoned as a juror —
should check with People Operations before assuming this policy applies,
since witness leave is handled separately.
