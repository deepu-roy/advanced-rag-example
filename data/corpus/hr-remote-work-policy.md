# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Northbridge Freight operates as a hybrid company: most corporate roles are
expected in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with Monday and
Friday available as remote days. Warehouse, dispatch, and on-site
operations roles follow their site's posted schedule instead, since those
roles require physical presence.

Employees may request a fully remote arrangement through their manager and
People Operations. Fully remote arrangements are evaluated based on role
suitability, not tenure, and are reviewed annually. Employees working
remotely, whether occasionally or fully, are expected to be reachable
during their normal working hours and to attend required meetings with
video on unless a specific exception is granted.

Employees relocating to a new state or country must notify People
Operations before the move, since it can affect payroll tax withholding,
benefits eligibility, and whether the role can legally remain remote from
the new location.
