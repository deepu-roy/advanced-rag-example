# Customer SLA Tiers

Northbridge offers three customer service level agreement (SLA) tiers:
Bronze, Silver, and Gold. Each tier defines a guaranteed on-time-in-full
delivery rate, a support response time, and a set of key performance
indicators (KPIs) reported to the customer monthly.

Bronze tier guarantees a 90% on-time-in-full rate and a 24-hour support
response time, with a quarterly KPI report. Silver tier guarantees a 95%
on-time-in-full rate and a 4-hour support response time, with a monthly
KPI report covering on-time performance, exception rate, and average
transit time.

Gold tier guarantees a 98% on-time-in-full rate and a 1-hour support
response time, with a weekly KPI report that adds two metrics Silver does
not receive: a named account manager's direct escalation line, and
proactive notification of any shipment at risk of missing its delivery
window before the customer has to ask. Gold tier customers are also
assigned dedicated carrier capacity during peak season, which Silver and
Bronze customers are not guaranteed.

Customers may upgrade tiers at any renewal date; downgrades take effect at
the start of the next contract term rather than immediately.
