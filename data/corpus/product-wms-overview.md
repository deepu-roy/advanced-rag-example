# Warehouse Management System Overview

The warehouse management system (WMS) is the internal platform used to
manage everything that happens inside a physical warehouse: receiving
inbound inventory, put-away, pick-and-pack for outbound orders, cycle
counting, and staging shipments for carrier pickup.

The WMS assigns each inbound pallet a bin location based on velocity
(how frequently that item ships) and generates optimized pick paths for
warehouse staff using a handheld scanner. It also drives the cycle-count
schedule, selecting a rotating sample of bin locations each day so that
the entire warehouse's inventory is verified on a regular cycle without
a full physical count.

Unlike the transportation management system, which coordinates carriers
and shipments across facilities, the WMS is scoped to a single facility at
a time — each warehouse runs its own WMS instance, though inventory
visibility is aggregated centrally for reporting.
