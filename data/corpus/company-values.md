# Company Values

Northbridge Freight operates by four stated values: Move With Urgency,
Own The Outcome, Show Your Work, and Respect The Dock.

Move With Urgency means treating a customer's shipment delay as everyone's
problem to solve quickly, not just the assigned coordinator's. Own The
Outcome means employees are expected to follow a problem through to
resolution rather than handing it off and considering their part done.
Show Your Work means decisions — especially ones affecting customers or
carriers — should be documented well enough that someone else could
understand the reasoning later.

Respect The Dock is specific to Northbridge's roots as a freight company:
it means every corporate employee, regardless of seniority, spends time in
a warehouse periodically to stay grounded in how operational decisions
actually play out on the floor. New corporate hires complete a half-day
warehouse shadow shift during onboarding as an introduction to this value.
