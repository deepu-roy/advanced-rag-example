# Company Holiday Calendar

Northbridge Freight observes ten paid company holidays each year for
corporate staff: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence
Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, and three
floating holidays employees may schedule at their own discretion during
the calendar year.

Warehouse and dispatch operations remain open on most holidays to serve
customer delivery commitments, following a separate holiday premium-pay
schedule rather than taking the day off. Operations staff who work a
recognized holiday receive 1.5 times their normal hourly rate for that
shift, in addition to accruing a floating holiday to use later.

Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year they are granted
and do not carry over, unlike PTO. Requests to use a floating holiday
follow the same approval process as a PTO request.
