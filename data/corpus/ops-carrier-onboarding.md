# Carrier Onboarding Checklist

Before a new carrier can be assigned any shipments, the carrier management
team completes a five-step onboarding checklist: verify active operating
authority with the relevant transportation authority, confirm minimum
cargo and liability insurance coverage, collect a signed rate agreement,
set up EDI or API connectivity for shipment tendering, and run three test
shipments end to end before going live.

Carriers offering both full truckload (FTL) and less than truckload (LTL)
service are onboarded separately for each service type, since the rate
agreements, capacity commitments, and performance thresholds differ
significantly between the two.

A carrier's onboarding is considered complete only once all three test
shipments succeed without a manual intervention. Carriers who fail two or
more test shipments are returned to the carrier management team for
additional setup review before a fourth attempt is scheduled.
