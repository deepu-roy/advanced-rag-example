# Device and BYOD Policy

Employees in corporate roles receive a company-managed laptop, which is
enrolled in mobile device management (MDM) and must not have its disk
encryption, MDM agent, or endpoint protection disabled. Warehouse and
dispatch roles are issued a company-managed tablet or handheld scanner
instead, configured for their specific site.

Personal devices (bring-your-own-device, or BYOD) may be used to access
email and calendar only, through the approved mobile app, which enforces a
passcode and remote-wipe capability on the corporate data container. BYOD
devices may not be used to access production systems, customer data, or
internal admin tools under any circumstances.

Lost or stolen devices, company-managed or BYOD, must be reported to IT
Security within one hour of discovery so the device can be remotely locked
or wiped. Employees are not held financially responsible for a lost
company-managed device if it is reported within this window.
