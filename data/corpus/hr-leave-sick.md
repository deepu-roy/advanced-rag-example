# Sick Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to ten (10) paid sick days per calendar year,
credited in full at the start of the year rather than accrued monthly.
Sick leave may be used for an employee's own illness or injury, to care for
an immediate family member who is ill, or to attend a medical, dental, or
mental health appointment.

Unused sick leave does not carry over to the following year and is not
paid out upon separation, in contrast to PTO. Employees who exhaust their
sick leave balance may use accrued PTO, or request an unpaid absence
through People Operations for extended illness.

For absences of three or more consecutive sick days, People Operations may
request a note from a healthcare provider before the employee returns to
work. Employees experiencing a serious health condition should also
contact People Operations about eligibility for a longer, job-protected
leave under applicable law, which is administered separately from this
sick leave balance.
