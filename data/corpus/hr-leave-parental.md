# Parental Leave Policy

Employees who become a parent through birth, adoption, or foster placement
are entitled to twelve (12) weeks of paid parental leave, at 100% of base
salary. This applies equally to primary and secondary caregivers, and to
employees of any gender. Parental leave may begin up to two weeks before
the expected birth or placement date and must be completed within twelve
months of the birth or placement.

To request parental leave, notify your manager and People Operations at
least 30 days before the expected start date when the timing is known in
advance (for example, a due date). For adoptions or placements with less
advance notice, notify People Operations as soon as the placement is
confirmed. People Operations will provide a leave plan confirming the
start date, duration, and return-to-work date.

Parental leave runs concurrently with any leave required under applicable
state or federal law, such as FMLA, rather than being added on top of it.
Employees may extend their leave using accrued PTO or an unpaid leave
extension, subject to manager approval. Health benefits continue
uninterrupted during parental leave.
