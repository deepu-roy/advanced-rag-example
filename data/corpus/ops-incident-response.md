# Operational Incident Logging and Communication

This document covers how operational incidents — a warehouse safety event,
a major delivery failure, or a facility issue like a power outage — should
be logged and communicated, as distinct from the on-call escalation
process used for software/systems incidents.

Every operational incident is logged in the Ops Incident Log within one
business day, including a description, the facility involved, and any
immediate corrective action taken. Incidents affecting customer-visible
service (for example, a facility outage that delays same-day shipments)
also require a customer-facing status update, drafted by the ops
communications lead and approved by the regional ops manager before
sending.

A weekly digest of the Ops Incident Log is shared with regional ops
managers every Monday, and any incident marked "recurring" is escalated
automatically to the process improvement team for root-cause review.
