# Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual

Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.25 days per
month, reaching 15 days per year during the first three years of
employment. Starting in the fourth year of employment, the accrual rate
increases to 1.67 days per month, or 20 days per year. Part-time employees
accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to their scheduled hours.

PTO accrues from an employee's first day and can be used as soon as it is
earned; there is no waiting period. Unused PTO carries over into the next
calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days; any balance above that cap is
paid out at the end of December at the employee's current daily rate.

To request PTO, submit a request through the HR portal at least five
business days in advance for anything longer than two consecutive days.
Manager approval is required and is based on team coverage, not on the
reason for the request — employees are never required to disclose why they
are taking PTO. Upon separation from the company, any accrued and unused
PTO is paid out in the final paycheck, in accordance with state law.
